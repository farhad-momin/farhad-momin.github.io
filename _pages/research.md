My research connects the seismic assessment of existing reinforced concrete buildings with data-driven methods for predicting structural and material behavior. Each area below lists the related publications.

## Seismic assessment of existing RC buildings

My M.Sc. thesis at BUET, supervised by Prof. Dr. Raquib Ahsan, compares seismic assessment methods for reinforced concrete buildings. Related work applies preliminary and detailed evaluation procedures, and retrofitting guidance, to existing buildings in Bangladesh.

### Related publications

- [Preliminary and detailed seismic evaluation of an existing RC building in Bangladesh: A case study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41062-024-01693-1), *Innovative Infrastructure Solutions*, 2024
- [CNCRP manual based seismic evaluation and retrofitting of existing RC buildings: A case study](https://doi.org/10.22075/jrce.2024.32938.1980), *Journal of Rehabilitation in Civil Engineering*, 2025
- [Seismic vulnerability assessment of an existing RC building in Bangladesh](https://iccesd.com/proc_2022/Papers/STE-4476.pdf), *ICCESD 2022*

## Machine learning for structural behavior

Data-driven models for the fundamental period of steel-braced RC frames. This work uses stacked generalization, deep symbolic regression and interpretable machine learning to identify which structural parameters matter most.

### Related publications

- [Data-driven approach to predict the fundamental period of steel-braced RC frames using stacked generalization machine learning models](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42107-023-00914-9), *Asian Journal of Civil Engineering*, 2023
- [Deep symbolic regression for numerical formulation of fundamental period in concentrically steel-braced RC frames](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42107-024-01076-y), *Asian Journal of Civil Engineering*, 2024
- [Comprehensive analysis of structural parameters influencing the fundamental period of steel-braced RC buildings using machine learning interpretability](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43503-025-00051-z), *AI in Civil Engineering*, 2025

## Machine learning for concrete and materials

Prediction of material properties from heterogeneous datasets, including UHPC compressive strength with an optimized NGBoost model, and steel corrosion rate in cementitious mortars with a Bayesian-optimized hierarchical mixture of experts.

### Related publications

- [Optimizing NGBoost with dynamic sequential model-based optimization for predicting UHPC compressive strength on heterogeneous datasets](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mtcomm.2025.112405), *Materials Today Communications*, 2025
- [Bayesian-optimized hierarchical mixture of experts for steel corrosion-rate prediction in cementitious mortars](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41024-025-00746-x), *Journal of Building Pathology and Rehabilitation*, 2026

## Recycled aggregate concrete

Sustainable construction materials, with a focus on how the properties of the parent concrete affect the compressive strength of recycled aggregate concrete.

### Related publications

- [Effect of parent concrete's properties on compressive strength of recycled aggregate concrete](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41024-025-00717-2), *Journal of Building Pathology and Rehabilitation*, 2026
