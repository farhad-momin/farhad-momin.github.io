I am a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at World University of Bangladesh and an M.Sc. candidate in Structural Engineering at BUET. My work brings together earthquake engineering, structural assessment, and data-driven methods.

My thesis compares seismic assessment methods for reinforced concrete buildings. I also apply machine learning to predict structural and material behavior, including the properties of recycled aggregate concrete. My aim is practical and reliable assessment of existing RC buildings in the context of seismic risk in Bangladesh.
